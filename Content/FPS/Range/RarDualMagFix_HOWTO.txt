RAR tactical dual mag fix — 2026-10-28
=====================================

Root cause
----------
1. SOCKET_Magazine_Reserve is on the GUN (SK_RAR Mesh1P), not the hand.
   Infima "two mags in hands" = WEP reload moves gun sockets to hands + both SM visible.
   BP_IG_AN_Magazine_Reserve_Set_Visiblity toggles weapon reserve via BPI_IG_Weapon —
   not a separate hand-attached mesh. Our pawn does not implement Infima BPI.
2. Last fix ScheduleInfimaMagazineNotifies HID the reserve at reload start, then hoped
   Infima PCH notify timers would re-show it. Project arms use AM_RAR_Reload (no those
   notifies); timer path left reserve hidden → only one mag near magwell.
3. Do NOT use DefaultSlot PlaySlotAnimationAsDynamicMontage for WEP (freezes mag bones).

Fix
---
- Remove Infima notify timer scheduling.
- PlayMagazineReload: bReloadReserveMagVisible=true, ShowReserveMagazineForReload()
  for the WHOLE reload (PolishHud behavior that worked before today's timer path).
- SetMeshVisibility: if flag set, re-show reserve after HideReserveMagazines.
- FinishMagazineReloadVisuals on AttachToOwingPawn (reload end / stop).
- PlayWeaponMontageOrSequence UNCHANGED.
