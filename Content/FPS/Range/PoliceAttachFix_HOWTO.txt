Police / Criminal weapon attach fix (1P + 3P + left hand)
=========================================================
Backup: Saved\CodeBackup_20261029_063214_PoliceAttachFix

Root causes
-----------
1) Bandits body/arms have hand_r but NO weapon_r / RifleSocket* / WeaponSocket.
2) Previous Ensure*GripSockets wrote identity (zero) stubs for weapon_r etc.
   ResolveAttachPoint preferred those stubs over real Ironbelly WeaponSocket /
   Manny RifleSocket* copies -> gun sat at hand pivot (1P wrong) or looked unmounted (3P).
3) Ensure skipped writing when a skeleton socket of the same name existed (never
   overrode with mesh-level sockets).
4) Weapon OverrideArms could swap Mesh1P off Bandits gloves after faction look,
   dropping the patched sockets (RAR/AK 1P both factions).
5) Left hand: many RAR/AK meshes have no FABRIK socket -> FABRIK_Alpha stayed 0.

What changed (.cpp only)
------------------------
- ShooterCharacter: EnsureMesh3PGripSockets / EnsureArms1PGripSockets upsert
  mesh-level sockets from live Manny / SK_Mannequin / Ironbelly; alias
  weapon_r/RifleSocketak/etc from best real source; NEVER write empty stubs.
- ShooterCharacter: CalculateFABRIKSocket blends alpha, respects reload +
  DisableLHandIK curve.
- Weapon: GetFabrikWorldTransform also tries LeftHandSocket/Grip and Root->Muzzle
  lerp for rifles; AttachToOwingPawn logs parent/socket ok + BAD attach warning.
- CombatComponent: while FactionLook Police/Criminal + bUseBanditsArms1P, lock
  Mesh1P to Bandits (ignore OverrideArms mesh).
