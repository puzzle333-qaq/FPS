Coop CQB — Hostile AI logic (MVP)
=================================

Behavior (AHostileAIController)
-------------------------------
States: Idle → Investigate → Combat → TakeCover

1) Pathfinding
   - Uses NavMesh FindPathToLocationSynchronously + AddMovementInput along path.
   - Goals: seen player, last-known position, noise investigate point, cover/peek points.
   - Bake RecastNavMesh on the map or AI will walk in straight segments only.

2) Hearing
   - Gunshots: CombatComponent Server_FireWeapon → AINoiseEvent::Report (loud, ~4500cm * loudness).
   - Footsteps: AI samples nearby player speed every 0.35s (sprint louder, crouch quieter, stand/creep ignored).
   - Heard noise → Investigate that point (unless already in combat with clear LOS).

3) Cover
   - On taking damage, or mid-combat (random prefer + under fire): 12-direction WorldStatic traces.
   - Picks a point just before a wall that blocks crouch-height LOS to the threat; projects to NavMesh.
   - TakeCover: crouch at cover → peek toward threat → shoot → duck back.

4) Crouch
   - Enabled on HostileAICharacter (NavAgent bCanCrouch).
   - Crouches while under fire, in cover, or holding cover; stands to peek/shoot lane.

Tune on controller defaults / Details
-------------------------------------
HearGunshotRange, HearFootstepRange, CoverSearchRadius,
LoseSightGrace, InvestigateTimeout, UnderFireDuration,
CoverHoldMin, PeekInterval, CombatPreferCoverChance

Character: SightRange, ShootRange, FireInterval, ShotDamage, MaxHealth

Debug tips
----------
- Empty graybox with no walls → cover search fails → stays in Combat (expected).
- No NavMesh → still moves toward goals but paths poorly.
- Log: [Hostile] death lines still fire; add temporary UE_LOG in SetState if needed.
